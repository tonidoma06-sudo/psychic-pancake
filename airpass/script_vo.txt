He paid $250,000 to fly free for the rest of his life… and the airline regretted it.

In 1987, Steven Rothstein bought a lifetime pass: unlimited first-class flights, anywhere, forever.

Two years later, he paid another $150,000 to bring a friend. Any friend. Every time.

So he flew. And flew. Over 10,000 flights.

He flew to London over and over, just because he could. He gave the extra seat to strangers.

The airline did the math. By some estimates, he was costing them millions.

In 2008, as he waited to board a flight to London, they told him his pass was cancelled. Forever.

They accused him of booking thousands of seats that went empty. He sued for $7 million.

Would you buy a lifetime pass?
