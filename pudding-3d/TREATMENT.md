# "The Pudding Guy": 3D Short (own style: Toy Theatre 3D, 1999)

Brief: vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 38.5 s, English, expressive AI voice-over (ElevenLabs). Written for retention: the
payoff is the first line, a new surprise every 3–5 s, numbers on screen, a punchline instead of a moral, and an ending
that loops into the opening (the overhead bin pops and the cups rain down → the avalanche).

**The first 3 seconds:** frame 1 is already in motion (cups pouring out of a garage onto a man in a polo shirt), the
stake is on screen from frame 1 ("12,000 PUDDING CUPS"), a brass stab hits on the first frame, and the first word is at
0.1 s. At 2.5 s a jumbo jet roars over the house while a counter races to 1,000,000+ MILES.
