# Almissa Digital: "Dobrodošli" (Silkscreen Travel Poster), test film, about 28 s

Brief (defaults, because this is a test run): English text on screen, **no voice-over** (the poster text is the subtitle, STYLE §4), 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 28 s. The score is a synthesized acoustic plucked guitar with cajón-style hits (Dalmatian *klapa*-adjacent folk feel, instruments only). No user assets: the place is drawn generically (cliffs, river mouth, an old stone tower). No real logos.

## Three candidate structures
1. **One poster, re-inked per language.** The same harbour poster is reprinted for each guest's language. The header in the sky changes: DOBRODOŠLI → WILLKOMMEN → WITAMY → BENVENUTI. Then the info band prints the offer. *The re-ink is the product: one site, many languages.*
2. **A street walk.** A multi-plane truck along the Omiš riva, each shop's sign printed as we pass (taxi, konoba, rafting), then the info band. Strong, but too many scenes for 28 s.
3. **A misprint story.** An old, out-of-date poster (wrong prices, Croatian only) drifts out of register, and the new pull lands true. Clever, but "out of date" needs words to read.

**Chosen: 1.** It is the one native move (palette re-ink) that *is* the selling point (multilingual), and it fits 28 s. The opening is the accent first (the sun alone on bare paper). The ending is the poster in use (WhatsApp booking stub printed last), then a pull-back lockup.

## Logline and arc
One small harbour poster learns to greet every guest in their own language, then tells the owner what it costs.
Setup (a bare sheet, one sun) → turn (the header re-inks into four languages) → ending (the registration snaps, the offer prints, the brand lockup).

## Benchmark
Learn from mid-century national-park screen-print series and contemporary single-template poster series: restraint, paper as a colour, and a new palette per print. Don't take any real poster's composition, lettering, park names or logos.

## Beat sheet / cue map
96 bpm (0.625 s per beat), 4/4. Bars: 0–4 intro (single plucks per pull), 4–10 groove (strum every 2 beats; the re-ink sweeps land on the downbeats of bars 6, 8, 10 → t≈ 9.4/11.9/14.4… adjusted to the visual grid), a 1-beat silence at the snap, bars 12–17 band (plucks on each printed item), the final chord on the stub.

## Titles
Condensed display (League Gothic / Big Shoulders style, OFL) for headers and values; Outfit 600, letter-spaced, for labels. Text inks are picked by contrast. Each header holds ≥ chars/15 + 1.5 s (readcheck).
