He tied 42 weather balloons to a lawn chair… and flew to 16,000 feet.

Larry Walters always wanted to fly. But the Air Force rejected him. Bad eyesight.

So he built his own aircraft: 42 helium balloons and a lawn chair.

He packed sandwiches, a radio… and a pellet gun. The plan: float for a while, then shoot a few balloons to come down.

The chair was tied to his Jeep. Then the rope snapped.

Larry shot straight up. Three miles high, freezing, in a lawn chair.

He drifted into the path of planes landing at LAX. Two pilots radioed the tower: there's a man in a lawn chair up here.

Larry pulled out the gun. Pop. Pop. Pop. Then he dropped it.

He came down… right into power lines, and blacked out an entire neighborhood.

Police were waiting. Asked why he did it, Larry said: "A man can't just sit around."

Would you get in the chair?
