# Facts in the film

| On screen / in the voice-over | Source |
|---|---|
| Larry Walters, a truck driver in Los Angeles (San Pedro), flew a lawn chair lifted by helium weather balloons on July 2, 1982 | Wikipedia, *Lawnchair Larry flight*; Ripley's |
| 42 balloons (accounts vary: 42, 43 or 45; Wikipedia gives 42) | Wikipedia; Ripley's |
| He had wanted to fly all his life; poor eyesight kept him from becoming an Air Force pilot | Wikipedia |
| He took sandwiches, a radio and a pellet gun; the plan was to float a while and shoot balloons to come down | Wikipedia; Migflug |
| The chair was tethered to his Jeep; the cord snapped and he shot up | Wikipedia |
| About 16,000 feet (an estimate: he had no proper altimeter) | Wikipedia; Guinness World Records |
| He drifted into the approach corridor of LAX; pilots of two airliners reported him to the tower | Wikipedia; Migflug |
| He shot several balloons, then dropped the gun | Wikipedia |
| He came down in power lines in Long Beach, causing a local blackout | Wikipedia; Migflug |
| He was arrested on landing; asked why, he said "A man can't just sit around." | Wikipedia; Plane & Pilot |

On screen, invented for the film: the house, the friends, the Air Force office, the pilots' faces, the family in the blackout, the airliner's livery (no airline is shown).
