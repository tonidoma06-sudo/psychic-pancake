# Adding your voice

1. Record the 8 lines below, word for word, with a short breath (about half a second) between lines. 30–35 s in total is the target pace; faster or slower is fine, the film follows you.
2. Send the file (wav, mp3 or m4a). Your voice only, no music.
3. I run `sh build.sh your-voice.wav`: every scene, hit and caption is re-timed to your words, the score is regenerated around them, and you get `rome-auction.mp4` (+ `rome-auction.srt`).

> In the year 193, the Roman Empire was sold… at an auction.
> The palace guards had just killed the emperor.
> Then they offered the throne to the highest bidder.
> The emperor's father-in-law offered every soldier 20,000 coins.
> A rich senator, Didius Julianus, bid 25,000.
> He won, and the Senate made him emperor.
> 66 days later, a rival general marched on Rome… and Julianus was executed.
> He bought an entire empire, and kept it for just 66 days.

Tips: a quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth, a bit more energy than normal speech. Say "193" as "one ninety-three".
