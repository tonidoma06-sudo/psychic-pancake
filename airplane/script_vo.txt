This man ate an entire airplane. Not a bite. The whole plane.

Michel Lotito was a French entertainer who could eat metal.

He cut it into tiny pieces, drank mineral oil, and washed it down with water. Up to two pounds a day.

He ate 18 bicycles. 15 shopping carts. 7 TVs. And a coffin… handles and all.

Then he started on a Cessna one-fifty. A real airplane. Piece by piece. It took him two years.

Doctors found his stomach walls were twice as thick as normal.

Guinness gave him a brass plaque for the strangest diet in the world. Legend says he ate that too.

And the only foods that made him sick? Bananas… and hard-boiled eggs.
