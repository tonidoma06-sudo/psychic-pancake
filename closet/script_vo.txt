Food kept disappearing from his kitchen. So he set up a hidden camera.

Japan, 2008. A man who lived alone noticed it first. A little food missing. Then more.

He wired a camera to his phone. One day at work, it buzzed: someone was walking through his house.

Police searched every room. Nobody. Then they opened the top shelf of a closet.

A woman was living in there. With a mattress. For about a year.
