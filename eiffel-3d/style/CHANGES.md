# Changes from v1 (rome-3d/style)
- Faces: from primitive eyes/lids/lips on a sphere to one sculpted head mesh with a painted, animated face (the v1 faces were too crude in close-ups).
- Bodies: painted tailoring on the torso, cloth textures, padded shoulders, open sleeves, larger heads (≈6.5 heads tall instead of 7).
- Acting: winks, blushes, reading, stamping, driving, waving, hiding the face; eased pose tracks with overshoot.
- Music: a lighter period caper instead of an epic, mixed 3 dB lower under the voice.
