# Adding your voice

1. Record the 9 lines below, word for word, with a short breath (about half a second) between lines. 40–45 s in total is the target pace; faster or slower is fine, the film follows you.
2. Send the file (wav, mp3 or m4a). Your voice only, no music.
3. I run `sh build.sh your-voice.wav`: every shot, gag and caption is re-timed to your words, the score is regenerated around them, and you get `eiffel-3d.mp4` (+ `eiffel-3d.srt`).

> In 1925, a man sold the Eiffel Tower… for scrap.
> Victor Lustig read that the tower was expensive to keep up.
> Posing as a government official, he invited scrap dealers to a secret meeting at a luxury Paris hotel.
> He told them the city was quietly selling the tower, so it had to stay secret.
> One dealer, André Poisson, wanted it so badly he paid, and even slipped him a bribe.
> Lustig fled with the money, and Poisson was too embarrassed to go to the police.
> So Lustig came back… and tried to sell it again.
> This time, a dealer went to the police, and Lustig had to run.
> If a deal is secret, urgent, and too good to miss, it's probably a scam.

Tips: a quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth, a bit more energy than normal speech. Say "1925" as "nineteen twenty-five", "André Poisson" as "on-DRAY pwah-SON".
The pauses after "Tower…" and "came back…" are where the gags land; leave a beat there.
