## What would flip a no

- Seeing other hosts on this coast whose Booking scores went up with it, with guest reviews actually praising the quick answers, and trying it free on one apartment before paying for all of them.
- Seeing it run in a comparable high-end villa where it clearly saved the owner messages while I'm abroad, without guests complaining it felt impersonal.
- Seeing it running for a whole season with hosts I know on this coast, and their review scores going the same or up, not down.
- If another host I know on the coast used it for a whole season and showed me it cut the night messages without guests complaining, and my son checked the GDPR agreement and said it is fine.
- If a neighbour host I trust used it a full summer and showed me guests still left good reviews and it saved them real hours, and my nephew could set it up for me.
- Seeing it running for a whole summer at a neighbour's apartment here in Omiš with no complaints, and being able to try it free in June before paying anything.
- If it were free or almost free for one small apartment, or a neighbour I trust used it and told me it brought better reviews or saved her real time.
- If another villa owner I respect on this coast used it all summer and told me her guests liked it and it only took the Wi-Fi and parking questions while I was abroad, I would try it for the weeks I'm away.
- A host I know on this coast using it for a whole season and telling me the guests liked it and it never embarrassed her.
- Seeing it running for a full season at two or three other villa owners I know on the coast, and hearing from them that guests liked it and it never embarrassed them.
- If I started getting a lot of messages at night or in languages I can't handle, so it was actually costing me sleep or reviews.
- If my cousin or a neighbour with the same kind of apartment used it for a full summer and showed me it got no complaints and saved real time, and the price was closer to €40 for the season.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
