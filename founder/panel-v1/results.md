## What would flip a no

- A host I know personally on the coast using it for a full season without problems, and a price for all my apartments together instead of per apartment.
- A neighbour with one apartment like mine using it for a whole season, with no mistakes and better reviews, and a price closer to €10 a month only in the summer months.
- If it were almost free for one apartment, like €5 to €10 a month with no setup fee, and only during July and August when I am too busy cleaning to answer.
- No setup fee and around €15 a month only in summer, plus seeing it answer my real guests correctly for a few weeks before I pay anything.
- If it was only for July and August for about €10 or €15 a month with no setup fee, and it really answered the Germans so I stop typing the same thing at 11 at night.
- Seeing it working for a full season at another high-end villa owner I know personally, with guests who were happy with it.
- Seeing other villa owners on this coast whose Booking scores went up with it, and dropping the €150 setup so I can test it for a full season with no money down.
- If my agency included it at no extra cost, or my grandson set it up and showed me that guests still came to me for the personal things.
- If I could try it a whole season and see that guests only use it for Wi-Fi and parking at night and still write to me for everything else, with no complaints in reviews.
- If I could see it working in a villa like mine for a season with good reviews mentioning it, and guests could still reach me directly with one tap whenever they want.
- A clear written GDPR agreement, and a free test through a full busy August month where I see it really takes the Wi-Fi and parking questions off my phone without guests feeling they talk to a machine.
- Seeing other high-end villa owners on this coast who used it a full season and kept or raised their Booking score, with guests mentioning it positively in reviews.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
