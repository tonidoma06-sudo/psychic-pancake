## 3. Positioning map

The axes are **done-for-you vs. do-it-yourself** (who writes the answers and connects WhatsApp) and **monthly price**.

- **DIY and free:** Airbnb and Booking.com auto-replies, the owner typing by hand, a paper manual.
- **DIY and cheap ($7–30):** HostBuddy, Touch Stay, Rentlio, Smoobu, HostReply, Hospitable. These are English-first SaaS tools and assume the owner uses a PMS.
- **DIY and mid-price ($39–300):** WATI, ManyChat, Tidio. These are generic builders, not made for tourism.
- **Done-for-you, one-off:** the Pula freelancer ($200), web agencies (€265–2,000).
- **Done-for-you, monthly, local and in person:** **empty except Almissa.**

## 4. Complaints, ranked

1. **Support is hard to reach (4 found).** Smoobu: "no phone support, no live chat, and emails are basically ignored" ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/smoobu.com?page=7)). A WATI reviewer called support "one of the worst" ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/customer-support/wati-profile/reviews/)).
2. **Billing, cancellation and price hikes (4 found).** WATI is "extremely difficult to cancel" ([Trustpilot](https://it.trustpilot.com/review/wati.io)) and "forces annual payments once you proceed to checkout" ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/204314/WATI/reviews/)). Hospitable (2 stars): "Price increase is bonkers", over AI features the reviewer did not use ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hospitable.com?page=7)).
3. **AI gets it wrong or puts guests off (4 found, mixed sources).** HostBuddy "can loop easily" and has referenced old details ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com.au/reviews/1067286/HostBuddy-AI)). G2's summary of Touch Stay flags "occasional unreliability of AI support" ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/touch-stay/reviews)). A guest: "ultimately ended up cancelling the booking as I was uncomfortable with it all" ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/airbnb-hosts-dont-want-to-talk-to-guests-anymore-are-outsourcing-messages-to-ai/)).
4. **Setup takes effort (2 found, thin).** HostBuddy: "you do need to invest some upfront time to properly set up your SOPs and messaging flows" ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10021407/HostBuddy-AI/reviews/)).
5. **Missing integrations (2 found, thin).** Rentlio: "very few channels to link with" ([Capterra IN](https://www.capterra.in/reviews/178760/rentlio)). Touch Stay: wants "additional integrations with property management systems" ([Capterra AU](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1062586/touch-stay)).

Review samples are small and skew positive (HostBuddy 5.0, Rentlio 98% positive). I found no reviews for any Croatian local provider.

## 5. The gap

The gap is **done-for-you setup, local and in person, with a monthly WhatsApp assistant that handles enquiries before anyone has booked.** The evidence:

- No Split, Omiš or Makarska provider of AI or WhatsApp assistants appeared in the searches. The nearest is a Pula freelancer selling a one-off build.
- Free native tools only reach guests who have already booked. Booking.com's Auto-Reply "mostly reach[es] a guest who has already booked" ([Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/opinion/4135090/read-before-the-guest-bookingcoms-ai-answers-before-you-can)). Taxi, transfer, tour and boat operators get direct WhatsApp enquiries that no OTA tool covers.
- The top complaints (unreachable support, billing traps, setup effort, wrong AI answers) all point to what a local person who sets it up, monitors it and bills a flat fee that can be paused would fix.

**Where there is no gap:** a plain FAQ assistant for apartments that are already on Booking or Airbnb. Free built-in tools and $7–19 SaaS tools cover it, and Almissa's €35 is hard to defend there unless the done-for-you service is the product.

## 6. The threat

- **Rentlio is the fastest copier.** It is Croatian, already sells AI guest communication from about €12 per month, and already has Croatian host relationships and integrations ([Chekin](https://chekin.com/en/blog/chekin-rentlio-integration/)). It could add a WhatsApp channel and outbound sales in the Split region within a season.
- **Booking.com and Airbnb** keep making auto-replies free and smarter ([Travolution](https://www.travolution.com/news/booking.com-launches-agentic-ai-tools/)). This shrinks the apartment use case.
- **Local web freelancers and agencies** can copy Almissa's stack in weeks, since Almissa itself builds with off-the-shelf AI tools. The Pula Contra listing at $200 shows this is already happening.
- **Apartmanica** could bundle an AI assistant into its websites for Croatian hosts.

The defensible parts are local presence, reference clients and reviews, and being the one person a non-technical owner can phone.
