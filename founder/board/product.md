# Product lens

Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong

- **The one-sentence pitch already exists.** "AI that answers your guests while you work" is clear, concrete and about the customer's day, not the technology.
- **The demo starts from the experience.** A live chat answering in German, Polish and Italian shows the owner the guest's moment before any explanation does.
- **The buying experience is low-friction.** Setup takes 1–2 weeks, the client tries it before paying, there is no long contract and the fee pauses off-season. That fits non-technical, seasonal owners.
- **Usage costs are included,** so the owner never sees a second bill from WhatsApp or an AI provider.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

1. **No focus.** Five product lines (guest assistant, WhatsApp booking, automations, websites, reviews) across apartments, taxis, tour operators, restaurants, clinics and trades, all from one person. Every extra product splits attention and dilutes the craft, so nothing gets done brilliantly. The guest assistant is the clear "one thing", and the rest compete with it.
2. **The seam between AI and owner.** The real experience happens when the AI is wrong or cannot answer: a wrong check-in time, a misquoted price, a booking summary the owner misses at 23:00. A guest who gets a confidently wrong answer in their own language blames the apartment, not the AI. Nothing in the brief says how hand-off, escalation or answer accuracy are handled.
3. **The proof does not match the product.** The only reference client is a taxi firm, and it got a website and a ride log, not the guest assistant. The demo apartment is fictional. Missing contact details on the site are a visible break in the craft at the very first touchpoint.

## The questions I would ask the founder

- If you could sell only one product next season, which is it, and what would you stop offering?
- Walk me through a German guest's first five minutes: scanning the QR code to getting the Wi-Fi password. Where can it go wrong?
- What happens when the assistant does not know the answer? Who hears about it, how fast, and does the guest know?
- How do you keep each property's answers correct when the owner changes the check-in time or the parking rules?
- Has any apartment owner used the guest assistant with real guests yet? (Unknown.)
- Did Taxi Omiš Almissa pay, and is it a related or family business? (Unknown.)
- How many hours a week can you give this, and how many clients can one person support well in July? (Unknown.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions

1. Lead with the AI guest assistant for apartment and villa owners only. Keep the other products off the front page until it works.
2. Put it live with at least 3 real properties and real guests, and show the logged questions, the wrong answers and how they were fixed.
3. Design and document the fallback path: what the guest sees and how the owner is alerted when the AI cannot answer.
4. Fix every visible seam before outreach: contact details on the site, a real (permitted) client in the demo, and a simple way for owners to update their own answers.
