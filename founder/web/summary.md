## Panel (20 simulated tourism-business owners)
**2 buy · 18 pass (10%)**. That's double the guest assistant's 5%, and 20% among villa owners who want direct bookings, but still an upper bound.
- Why they pass: **need 8** ("Google Maps and WhatsApp already bring guests"), **price 6** (€600 up front in November, no proof of bookings), trust 2.
- What would flip a no: proof from a nearby business that a site brought direct bookings or cut platform commission (most common); **pay half now, half after the season / after the first booking** (6 owners asked for this); a cheaper one-page site for single apartments (~€300); a written promise they can take the domain and site with them.
- Price: acceptable range **€200–€700**, bargain/expensive crossover at about €400, median "too expensive" €800. **€600 is inside the range, near the top.**

## Changes to make the offer sell
1. **50% to start, 50% at go-live (or before 1 May).** This is the most-asked flip, and it costs you nothing if the deposit covers your time.
2. **"Your domain, your site" in writing:** a transfer guarantee if they leave the care plan.
3. **Lead with villa owners who want direct bookings, and operators.** Skip restaurants (0/5: "people read Maps, not websites") and single apartments, or give them a €300 one-page package.
4. **Proof first:** the Taxi Omiš Almissa site plus 2 demo sites, with a before/after Google Maps result, once you have one.
5. Pitch "a few direct bookings pay for it": at 15% Booking commission, 2–3 direct villa weeks cover €600.
