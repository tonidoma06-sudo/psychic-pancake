## What would flip a no

- Seeing a few sites they already built for other konobas or businesses near me and hearing from one of those owners that changes really get done in two days.
- If they took a smaller deposit and the rest only after the site brought me a few direct bookings, or showed me a villa owner nearby who saved more than €600 in Booking commission in one season with it.
- If a colleague on the coast showed me in real numbers that a website brought him more bookings, or if you fix my Google Maps first and I see the calls go up before paying for the site.
- Pay half in spring and half after the first season, or show me a couple of transfer guys on this coast who got real extra bookings from it, plus a clear written promise I can take the site and domain elsewhere if I stop paying.
- Proof from another villa or apartment owner nearby that their site brought real direct bookings in the first season, enough to save more than it cost in Booking commission.
- Seeing two or three sites they already made for konobas or restaurants near Omiš or Makarska, and hearing from one of those owners that changes really happen within two days.
- Seeing another taxi or boat guy on this coast whose new site actually brought him more WhatsApp bookings, or paying half now and half only after the bookings come.
- Another taxi or rafting guy from Omiš showing me his WhatsApp bookings went up and his commission bill went down after the site, or paying part of it only after it brings me bookings.
- If a konoba near me showed me real numbers, more reservations after their site went up, or if you did the Google Maps tidy-up first and took the €600 only once I saw more calls and messages.
- A smaller one-apartment package around €300, or splitting the €600 into payments before the season starts.
- If a villa owner near me showed me they really got direct bookings from a site like this in one season, and my daughter said she could send guests to it from Instagram.
- Seeing another villa owner nearby who actually gets direct bookings from their site, and paying part of the €600 only after it brings me a booking or two.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
