# Marketing: Almissa Digital, season 2027

The evidence behind this plan: the panels (`panel-v1/`, `panel/`), `competitors.md`, `offer.md`, `pricing.md` and `cfo.md`. Panel quotes are research and never go into ads.

## 1. Who to talk to first (from the panels)

- **Hosts with several properties who are overwhelmed in peak season** (2 of 3 buyers across both panels), and **villa owners who live abroad** (1 of 3). Both buy for convenience.
- **Taxi, transfer, tour and boat operators** who take enquiries on WhatsApp. This is the gap in `competitors.md`: nobody covers enquiries before a booking is made. Not panel-tested yet.
- **Not now:** single-apartment family hosts (0 of 24 bought across both panels; "I live next door", "Booking already does it").

## 2. Positioning

1. *For hosts with 3 or more apartments who answer the same German and Polish questions at midnight in August, Almissa Digital is the guest assistant set up in person in Omiš that answers only the routine questions and passes every real conversation to you, unlike booking-platform auto-messages that guests stop reading after check-in.*
2. *For taxi, transfer and tour operators who lose enquiries on WhatsApp while they are driving, Almissa Digital is the WhatsApp booking assistant that answers, quotes your price list and collects the details before anyone has booked, unlike booking platforms that only talk to guests after they book.*
3. *For small tourism businesses on the Omiš–Makarska coast, Almissa Digital is the local person who sets up AI for you and answers the phone when something breaks, unlike software you have to configure yourself.*

**Recommended: run 1 and 2 as two separate campaigns, with 3 as the trust line under both.** The evidence: 18 of 60 panel passes were about trust (one-person company, a wrong answer means a bad review), and the only proof hosts would accept was "a host I know, for a full season". Positioning 3 answers that, but only in person.

|---|---|---|---|
| **In-person visits with a live demo on your phone** (host clusters in Omiš, Duće, Lokva Rogoznica, the Makarska Riviera; taxi ranks and tour kiosks) | Trust only comes from a person and a neighbour. | **Local host Facebook groups and the town tourist-board host meetings** (find the active iznajmljivači groups for Omiš, Split and Makarska; ask the TZ about pre-season host education days) | Where hosts already ask each other about eVisitor, Booking and prices | €0, plus up to €300 of boosted posts | WhatsApp enquiries per post. **Not doing, and why:** TikTok and Instagram ads (hosts don't buy B2B services there, and it reaches guests, not owners), Google Ads (nobody searches for "AI guest assistant Omiš"), cold email (trust problem), and anything aimed at single-apartment hosts this season.

### Ten hooks

1. "Wi-Fi password, check-in, parking. Your German guests get the answer at 23:40, and you sleep." *(the overwhelmed host)*
2. "It answers only the routine questions. Every real conversation still comes to you." *("guests want me, not a robot")*
3. "When it isn't sure, it doesn't guess. It sends the question to your WhatsApp." *("wrong answer, bad review")*
4. "You approve every answer before a guest sees it." *("wrong answer")*
5. "€120 for the whole season. Nothing in winter." *("another subscription")*
6. "One price for all your apartments, not €35 each." *(the per-property objection)*
7. "Signed GDPR agreement. Your guests' data stays in the EU." *(GDPR)*
8. "Made in Omiš. I'll come and set it up in your kitchen." *(trust in a one-person company)*
9. Operators: "Enquiries come in while you're driving. Who answers?" *(the pre-booking gap)*
10. Operators: "Price, time, pick-up, flight number, collected before you even pick up the phone." *(the WhatsApp booking assistant)*

## 5. Budget and numbers

**Budget (pre-season, €300 in ads, from `numbers.json`):** €150 boosted posts in local groups/pages, €100 printed one-pagers and QR samples, €50 referral rewards.

**The most you can pay to win a customer:**
- Guest assistant: contribution €16.90 × 6 season months = **€101 per property per season**. Panel buyers start with 1 property. **Cap acquisition cost at about €30 per property** (a third of first-season value), because renewal is unproven.
- WhatsApp booking assistant at €79: €44.20 × about 7 months + €300 setup ≈ **€610 in year 1. Cap at about €150 per operator.**

**Three numbers to watch every week:**
| number | change something if |
|---|---|
| Signed pre-orders per 10 in-person visits | below 1 after 30 visits |
| Properties signed versus the 40-property cap | below 10 by 15 March |
| WhatsApp enquiries per Facebook post | below 2 after 4 posts |
