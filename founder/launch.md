# Launch plan: Almissa Digital, season 2027

Today is 9 November 2026. **Public launch is Monday 1 March 2027.** The pre-season offer deadline is 31 March. Every client is live before 1 May. The dates come from the season: hosts and operators decide in Feb–Mar and are too busy from May on.

## 1. Test before you spend (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)

The panel said 5% (an upper bound). Real buyers decide. **Two cheap tests, in parallel:**

| test | how | success line, written down now | if it falls short |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Paid pilots: WhatsApp booking assistant** | Offer 3 taxi, transfer, tour or boat operators (not Taxi Omiš Almissa) a pilot at €79/month + €150 setup (half price), paid up front for March–May | **3 paying operators by 31 January** | Fewer than 2: run `/founder-consumer` on operators and rethink with `/founder-offer` before spending on ads |
| **B. Founding hosts: guest assistant** | 30 in-person conversations with hosts who have **3 or more properties**, or villa owners who live abroad. Offer the season pass with a refundable €30 deposit, alternating €120 and €90 (`pricing.md`) | **6 deposits from 30 conversations (20%)**, at least 3 at €120 | Fewer than 3: stop selling the guest assistant as a product; keep it as a free add-on for operators and website clients |

Plus: **5 free Pioneer installs** at well-known hosts (in exchange for showing a neighbour and an honest August report). That's the social proof the panel asked for.

## 4. The first 30 days (1–31 March) and the first season

**Weekly numbers (every Friday):**
| number | target | change something if |
|---|---|---|
| Properties signed (versus break-even of 26 and the cap of 40) | 10 by 15 Mar, 20 by 31 Mar | under 10 by 15 Mar |
| Operators on the WhatsApp booking assistant (break-even 10 at €79) | 5 by 31 Mar | under 3 |
| Deposits per 10 in-person visits | 1 or more | under 1 after 30 visits |
| WhatsApp enquiries per Facebook post | 3 or more | under 2 after 4 posts |

**Reviews:**
- **Day 7 (8 Mar):** which hook and which segment produced deposits? Which objection came up most? Is it the same as the panel's (trust)?
- **Day 14 (15 Mar):** under 10 properties? Move all selling time to operators and multi-property hosts, and use the €90 price if test B favoured it.
- **Day 30 (31 Mar):** total signed versus break-even. If properties + operators can't cover about €431/month of fixed costs by July, plan winter income from website projects and confirm the second-activity contribution setup with the accountant.
- **After the season (1 Nov 2027):** renewal intent, August reports, real buy rate versus the panel's 5%. Re-run `/founder-plan` with the real numbers.
