# Pricing: Almissa Digital AI guest assistant

All amounts are in euros. The tools print "$", but every input was in €.

## 1. The price

**€120 per property for the season (May–October), which works out to €20 a month. No setup fee if bought by 31 March. Each extra property is €60 a season.**

Why this price:
- **What buyers said** (`pricing-curve.md`, 40 simulated hosts): the acceptable range is **€8–€25 a month**, the point of least resistance is €10, and the point where as many hosts call it a bargain as call it expensive is €15. The current €35 + €150 setup is **above the range**. For the median host, €35 is exactly "too expensive, I would not buy."
- **What competitors charge** (`competitors.md`): self-serve guest assistant tools cost about $7–$29 per property per month, with a median of about $19. Rentlio, a Croatian property-management system, sells AI guest messaging from about €12. Airbnb and Booking.com auto-replies are free. At €20 a month, Almissa sits near the competitor median while doing the setup in person, which is the one thing nobody else does.
- **What the business needs** (CFO tool, the same costs at every price): €3.10 of variable cost per property-month and about €420 a month of fixed costs, which are estimates.

| price per property-month | contribution | break-even (active properties) | margin at 40 properties | year 1 (with the season ramp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| €15 | €11.90 | 36 | 9% | −€3,005 |
| **€20** | **€16.90** | **25** | **32%** | **−€2,150** |
| €25 | €21.90 | 20 | 46% | −€1,295 |
| €35 | €31.90 | 14 | 61% | +€415 (but the panel says few will buy at €35) |

**The honest result:** no price inside the range buyers accept makes the guest assistant alone profitable in year 1. It runs at a loss through the winter months. The assistant has to be the way in that leads to higher-value work. That means the review booster, the WhatsApp booking assistant for taxi, tour and boat operators, and websites. The ladder below does that.

## 2. The ladder

| rung | what's in it | price | reason to step up |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Good: Guest assistant** | QR/link assistant in the guest's language, questions it can't answer forwarded to your WhatsApp, answers edited in a table | €120 per property per season (May–Oct); +€60 per extra property | Hosts with several properties pay much less per property. 8 panel members asked for one price covering all their properties. |
| **Better: Guest assistant + Google reviews** | The above, plus an automatic review request after checkout and a suggested reply to every review | €180 per property per season | Review-focused hosts and villa owners: "a 9.2 vs 9.5 matters". |
| **Best: WhatsApp booking assistant** (taxi, transfers, tours, boats, restaurants) | Answers enquiries before anyone has booked, quotes from your price list, collects booking details, sends you a summary to confirm | €60 a month + €300 setup (keep the current price) | This is the gap in `competitors.md`: no platform tool covers enquiries before booking, and paid WhatsApp bot tools cost $39–$299 plus Meta's message fees. |

Websites (from €400) and automations (from €150) stay as one-off projects.

## 3. The opening offer

**Pre-season offer: book by 31 March 2027 and the setup is free**, normally €75 per property after that date. The deadline is real because setup capacity runs out before May. This answers the most repeated "what would flip a no" answer, *no setup fee*, without discounting the season price itself.

## 4. What to test with real hosts

Two prices inside the range:
- **A: €120 a season** (€20 a month)
- **B: €90 a season** (€15 a month)

How to test: during in-person visits in Feb–Mar, alternate A and B on a printed one-page offer, about 20 hosts each. Count signed pre-orders, not "sounds good". In parallel, run two Facebook ads to two landing pages with the two prices and count WhatsApp enquiries. If B more than doubles sign-ups, B wins. Otherwise keep A.

## 5. Price objections from the panel, word for word

1. "I have one apartment next to my house and my guests ask the same three questions, which I answer myself on my phone, even at night. Paying €150 plus €35 a month for a machine to tell them the Wi-Fi password makes no sense when I earn so little from it." (P003)
2. "With four apartments that's €140 a month plus €600 setup for questions I already answer for free." (P014)
3. "€35 every month per apartment plus €150 to start is real money on my income, and my laminated sheet plus the free Booking and Airbnb messages already answer the Wi-Fi and check-in questions." (P031)

These are simulated buyers. Use their answers to choose what to test, not as proof.
