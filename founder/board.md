# Board summary: Almissa Digital

## The vote
**3 FUND IF, 0 FUND, 0 PASS. Average score: 5.0 / 10** (Offers 6, Product 5, Monopoly 4)

## Risks raised by more than one member
1. **Too many products for one person.** All three lenses flagged this. Five service lines are sold to six types of business. Offers sees a menu that looks like a commodity. Monopoly sees a generalist agency that doesn't compound over time. Product sees divided attention, with nothing done brilliantly.
2. **Proof is weak and doesn't match the product.** All three: the only reference client, Taxi Omiš Almissa, may be related to the founder and may not have paid. It received a website and a ride log, not the guest assistant. The demo apartment is fictional.
3. **Thin, seasonal, low-budget market, and its size is unknown.** Offers and Monopoly: a €35/month fee that pauses off-season may bring in only a few hundred euros per client per year. Nobody has counted the target businesses, the close rate or the founder's hours.
4. **Free alternatives and copycats.** Offers and Monopoly: booking-platform auto-messages, WhatsApp Business quick replies, freelancers and agencies using the same AI tools. There is no proprietary edge.

The members disagree on one point. Offers would keep the bundle and raise the price (assistant + WhatsApp + reviews sold as one package). Monopoly and Product would cut down to the **guest assistant for apartment and villa owners only**. Pricing, the offer and the CFO steps should settle this with numbers.

Only Product raised one further risk: **what happens when the AI gets it wrong.** A guest who gets a wrong answer in their own language blames the host.

## The strongest version the board can see
This is narrower than what the website pitches. It's a **local, done-for-you AI guest assistant for apartment and villa owners on the Omiš to Makarska coast**. It's sold face to face before the season, live on real properties with logged results, and paired with a review booster because hosts live on reviews. It's priced as a single package worth more than €35/month. The taxi, restaurant, clinic and website work becomes custom projects taken on when there's spare capacity, not the headline. The edge is being the person in town who sets it up, keeps it correct and answers when something breaks, which no global software tool does.
