# Almissa Digital: one page

**Verdict: Not yet** (computed by `compile.py`: year 1 makes a loss; 5% of simulated buyers buy, against a 25% bar)

**The problem.** Small tourism businesses on the Omiš–Makarska coast answer the same guest questions in German, Polish and Italian, often at night and in peak season. Taxi, transfer and tour operators lose enquiries on WhatsApp while they're driving or out on the water. No booking platform answers enquiries before a booking is made.

**The customer.** Taxi, transfer, tour and boat operators come first. Then hosts with 3 or more properties, and villa owners who live abroad. *Not* single-apartment hosts (0 of 24 bought on the panel).

**The offer and price.**
- **"Mirni Kolovoz" Season Pass: AI guest assistant.** €120 per property per season (May–Oct), extra properties +€60. No setup fee until 31 March. GDPR agreement, August report, 30-day refund guarantee.
- **WhatsApp booking assistant for operators.** Recommended at €79/month + €300 setup (currently €60 on the site).
- Websites from €400, automations from €150, as projects.

Whole business, worked out by hand (guest assistant + 8 operators + setup fees + 4 websites): **about +€1,160 in year 1, before any founder pay.**

**The evidence.**
- Panels: 2 of 40 bought at €35/month; 1 of 20 bought the improved €120-per-season offer. Price objections fell, but trust and need didn't.
- Competitor gap: no local, in-person, done-for-you provider. Nothing covers WhatsApp enquiries before a booking. Self-serve guest tools cost about $7–$29 per property per month (`competitors.md`).
- Real test: **not yet run.** Paid operator pilots and host deposits are planned for Jan–Feb 2027 (`launch.md`).

**What the money is for.** About €700 in startup costs (QR printing, flyers, €300 of pre-season ads, registration). A winter cash cushion of about €2,000–€4,000 for fixed costs, mostly obrt contributions of €290.98/month.

**The ask.** No outside money is needed. The asks are: **3 paying operator pilots by 31 January 2027**, **6 host deposits from 30 conversations by 15 February**, and an accountant's answer on main versus second-activity obrt.
