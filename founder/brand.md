# Brand: Almissa Digital

The brand is built on the positioning in `marketing.md`: **the local person who sets up AI for you and answers when something breaks.** The panel bought only on convenience and refused on trust, so the brand's job is to be trusted, not to look clever.

## 1. The name

The working name **Almissa Digital** is already on the website, the logo concepts and the ads. "Almissa" is the old Latin name of Omiš.

**A conflict found in public search.** "Almissa" is already busy in Omiš tourism, which is exactly the market Almissa Digital sells to:
- **ALMISSA-LARUS d.o.o.**, Omiš, registered 2009, with tourism, hospitality and travel-agency activities ([companywall.hr](https://www.companywall.hr/tvrtka/almissa-larus-doo/MM1X1haq))
- **Camp Almissa**, the largest local campsite, renamed from Galeb in 2025 ([campertunity](https://campertunity.com/listings/campgrounds/camp-almissa-omis.md))
- **Taxi Omiš Almissa**, your own reference client, and a holiday house called "ALMISSA"

So the risk is **medium**. A place name is hard to own, and a host could think Almissa Digital belongs to the campsite company. The upside is that it reads as "from here", which is the trust signal the panel asked for.

### Ten candidates

| Mirni Kolovoz | evocative | "Quiet August": the outcome | Lovely in HR, hard for foreigners / good / weak icon | Better as an offer name (already used for one) than a company name |
| Domaćin AI | descriptive | "The host's AI" | Clear in HR / ok / "D" | Generic, hard to protect |
| Lučica Lab | evocative | "Little harbour" | Soft and memorable / good / boat icon | Used as the fictional demo apartment's name. Pick one use. |
| Cetina Digital | place | The local river | Good / good / wave icon | A river name, so others likely use it. Check. |
| Bura Bot | evocative | The north wind: fast | Fun / good / a swirl | "Bot" plays into the "robot" objection |
| [Founder's name] AI | founder | A person behind it | Personal / ok / "T" | Hard to sell the business later; uses the founder's name |
| Pitaj Domaćina | descriptive | "Ask the host" | Clear in HR / long / weak | Generic |
| Kampanel | invented | A bell tower, the local landmark | Neutral / good / bell icon | Invented, needs explaining |
| Almissa Lab | place | Same as above, less "agency" | Same / same | Same name conflict |

**Shortlist:** 1. **Almissa Digital** (keep it: everything already uses it and it says "local"), 2. Cetina Digital, 3. Lučica Lab.

**Recommendation: keep Almissa Digital.** Before printing anything, run the checks below. Always write it with "Digital" so it can't be mistaken for the campsite or travel company.

### Checks you need to run yourself
Public searches only. A trademark lawyer confirms the result, and nothing is yours until you register it.
- **Trademark:** search the EU register (EUIPO eSearch, https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/), Croatia's DZIV register (https://www.dziv.hr/en/), and WIPO's Global Brand Database (https://branddb.wipo.int/) for "ALMISSA". Check classes **42** (software/SaaS), **35** (business services/advertising) and **43/39** (accommodation/travel, where the campsite company would sit).
- **Domains:** this session couldn't reach the domain registry lookup. Check `almissa.digital`, `almissadigital.hr` and `almissadigital.com` at a registrar, or via CARNET's .hr WHOIS (https://www.dns.hr/).
- **Handles:** `@almissadigital` on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. Also check WhatsApp Business display-name approval.
- **Confusion with competitors:** none of the competitors in `competitors.md` uses a similar name. The confusion risk is local (above).

## 2. Voice and promise

**Promise:** *"Your guests get answers. You keep the conversations that matter. And I'm a phone call away."*

**Taglines (choose one):**
1. "AI koji odgovara gostima, dok vi radite." / "AI that answers your guests while you work." (already on the site)
2. "Rutina za AI. Gosti za vas." / "The routine for the AI. The guests for you."
3. "Lokalno postavljeno. Uvijek dostupno." / "Set up locally. Always reachable."

Option 2 answers the top objection ("my guests want me, not a robot") and is the one we recommend.

**Voice:**
| adjective | do | don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Neighbourly** | "I'll come to you in Duće and set it up at your kitchen table." | "Our team delivers end-to-end AI solutions." |
| **Plain** | "When it isn't sure, it sends the question to your WhatsApp." | "Smart escalation with intelligent fallback." |
| **Honest** | "It handles the Wi-Fi and parking questions. Broken air-con still comes to you." | "Never answer a guest again!" |

**Before and after**, from `pitch-v1.md`:
- Before: "an AI guest assistant for your holiday apartment. Guests … get instant answers in their own language."
- After: "Your guests get the Wi-Fi password at midnight in German. Every real conversation still comes to you."

## 3. The look (keep what the website already has)

| colour | hex | job | contrast check (WCAG) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adriatic navy | `#0E3B5C` | Main background, headings | White text 11.7:1 ✓ AAA; yellow text 7.6:1 ✓ AAA |
| Taxi-sign yellow | `#FFC93C` | Calls to action, highlights | Deep-navy `#0A2A42` text on yellow 9.6:1 ✓ AAA |
| Sea mist | `#EEF3F6` | Page background | Ink `#0C2233` 14.5:1 ✓; muted `#4F6577` 5.4:1 ✓ AA |
| (WhatsApp green `#1FAF58`) | – | WhatsApp button only | White on it is **2.9:1 ✗**. Use 18px+ bold text, or darken to about `#14803F`. |

**Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display) and Figtree (text). Both are free under the SIL Open Font License from Google Fonts, and both are already on the site.

**Logo brief:** the wordmark "**Al**missa" with "Al" in yellow, the four-point star, and "DIGITAL" in small letter-spacing (as on the site and in the logo-ideas artifact). It must work on a QR card in an apartment, a car door magnet, a WhatsApp profile picture at 32px (use the star alone, or "A" plus the star), and a printed A4 one-pager. Avoid robots, chat bubbles with faces, and anything that looks like the campsite's branding. The existing logo ideas are **concepts**, not a finished logo.

## 4. The first five touchpoints

1. **The printed one-pager / homepage:** the promise, a QR you can scan to try the demo, one price, your face and phone number. Contact details are currently missing on the site. Fix this first.
2. **The QR card in the apartment** (the "packaging"): "Questions? Scan for instant answers in your language. For anything else: [host's name] on WhatsApp." The host's name comes first, not Almissa.
3. **The setup confirmation** (WhatsApp): "Gotovo ✔. Your assistant is live at Apartmani ___. Here are the 24 answers you approved. Send me any change and I'll update it within 24 hours. – [your first name]"
4. **The first social post:** a Pioneer host's question of the week (with their permission), not an ad.
5. **The reply to the first complaint:** "You're right, it gave the wrong check-out time. I fixed it at 9:12, and here's the corrected answer. If you want the 30-day refund, it's yours, no questions asked."
