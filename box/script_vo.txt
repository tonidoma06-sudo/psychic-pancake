He mailed himself across the world… in a wooden box.

1964. Reg Spiers, an Australian javelin thrower, was stuck in London. Broke. And his daughter's birthday was coming.

So a friend nailed him into a crate and shipped it as air cargo. Cash on delivery.

Inside: a pillow, a flashlight, some canned food… and a bottle to pee in.

Sixty-three hours later, the crate landed in Perth. He climbed out, walked out of the airport… and made it home.
